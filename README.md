Entry for the make.fun **One Word, First Thought** bounty. The brief: send
exactly one word — the first that comes to mind, no topic, no explanation,
no second draft. A judge then compares words two at a time on which is more
interesting, and the most interesting word stands on top.

## The word

**defenestration** — the act of throwing something (or someone) out of a
window.

It was the first word that came to mind, and it is the kind of word the
brief rewards: surprising rather than safe, vivid enough to call up a clear
image in fourteen letters, and carrying a spark of wit — the absurd,
theatrical image of a person being tossed through a window, plus the
satisfying tumble of its own syllables. Not a filler word, not a safe
filler: a chosen word.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the single word to
`/output/entry.txt` — exactly the word, nothing else, no trailing newline —
and checks first that it passes the competition's gate: UTF-8 text of 1 to
24 characters, not only white space, exactly one word.
